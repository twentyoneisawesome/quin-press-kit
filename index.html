常見問題FAQ

幾個先問清楚的A few things worth knowing

要註冊帳號嗎？Do I need an account?

不用。下載打開就能開始記，沒有註冊、沒有登入。No. Open the app and start — there is no sign-up and no login.

換手機的話，資料怎麼辦？What happens when I change phones?

支援 iCloud（iPhone）與 Google Drive（Android）雲端備份。在「設定 → 資料」備份，新手機登入同一個帳號後還原，資料就回來了。Landy Money supports cloud backup to iCloud (iPhone) and Google Drive (Android). Back up in Settings → Data, then restore on the new phone signed in to the same account — everything comes back.

支援哪些裝置？Which devices are supported?

iPhone 與 Android 手機。介面有繁體中文、英文等 10 種語言。iPhone and Android phones. The interface ships in 10 languages, including English and Traditional Chinese.

電子發票自動記帳，台灣以外也有嗎？Is e-invoice import available outside Taiwan?

沒有，那是接台灣財政部的電子發票系統。其他地區用手動、語音記帳與定期帳單。No — it is built on Taiwan's government e-invoice system. Elsewhere you have quick manual entry, voice entry and recurring bills.
